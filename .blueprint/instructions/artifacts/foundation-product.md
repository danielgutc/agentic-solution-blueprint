# Foundation Artifact: product.md

## Purpose

Define enduring business context and intended outcomes before technical decomposition.

## Canonical structure

- Use `.blueprint/templates/foundation/product.template.md`.

## Authoring rules

- Keep language business-facing and stable across releases.
- Make actors explicit and outcome-oriented.
- Keep goals measurable; keep non-goals explicit.
- Capture assumptions that influence requirements or architecture.

## Quality checklist

- Problem statement is clear and bounded.
- Core outcomes are testable at business level.
- Goals and non-goals do not conflict.
